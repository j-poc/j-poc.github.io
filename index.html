How it works

  1. Enter your website

    We ask AI the questions your buyers ask, twice each, and show how often you are named and who is named instead. The first check is free.

  2. Unlock the full report

    See every question, every page AI relied on and every fix. Cancel any time.

  3. Connect your site once

    WordPress, GitHub or a webhook. We write the fixes and, unless you chose Review first, send the checked changes for your own site. Then we check each one is really there.

  4. See if it worked

    Each week we compare the questions we worked on with the ones we left alone, and tell you plainly if it worked.

Read the full steps

Half your questions are left alone

Most tools hand you a to-do list. Holdout splits your buyer questions in two at random on day one. We work on one half and leave the other half untouched. AI answers drift from week to week on their own, so the half we left alone shows that drift. Whatever the worked-on half gains beyond it is the lift. If both halves move the same, we tell you: no lift detected.

Read the method

Autopilot, or review first

Autopilot (the default)

Once your site is connected, Holdout sends the first fixes, then up to 5 more each week (15 on Holdout Growth). A fix goes out only when every number in it has a source. Every change is logged, and you can undo it or pause Autopilot at any time.

Review first (if you prefer)

Holdout writes each fix and waits. You read it, see where every fact came from, and approve it. Nothing goes live until you say so.

In both modes, messages to other websites stay as drafts. We never send anything in your name. A fix counts as live only when we open your public page and find its text.

What we will and will not tell you

  • How often, out of how many. Every number comes with the count behind it and a likely range.
  • Which AI, and how we asked. Answers from an API can differ from the app you use. Every report says which one we used.
  • When nothing moved. You hear it from us first.
  • No rank from one answer. One answer is close to a coin flip.
  • No promise that AI will name you. Nobody controls that. We promise the work ships and the measurement is honest.
  • No busywork. We only do work with evidence behind it. We do not sell schema markup or an llms.txt file as fixes.
