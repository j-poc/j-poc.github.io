# Tools for measuring competitor recommendations in AI answers

Holdout is a planned tool that measures how often your business is named in AI answers and which competitors are named instead. It has not launched, so it cannot be bought today; the source does not identify other tools. [1]

## What Holdout measures

Holdout asks AI the questions your buyers ask, twice each. Its report shows how often you are named, who was named instead and how often, and which pages the AI relied on, with links. [1]

> “When buyers ask AI who to choose, are you named?” — Holdout homepage [1]

## What a check can show

The homepage shows an illustrative report with “Named in 3 of 16 answers” and “19%, likely range 7% to 43%.” Holdout says this is an example of the layout, not a real result; a real report would show your own numbers, the engine asked, and how it was asked. [1]

## How Holdout checks whether changes helped

Holdout randomly splits buyer questions into a worked-on half and an untouched half. Each week it compares them to account for changes in AI answers over time. If both halves move the same, Holdout says “no lift detected.” [1]

## Who it suits, and who it does not

It may suit a business that wants competitor mentions tracked alongside evidence-based website changes. It is not a fit for someone who needs a tool they can buy now: Holdout has not launched and has no customers. It also does not promise AI will name a business. [1]

## Planned pricing

The stated planned prices are $199 a month for Holdout, $599 a month for Holdout Growth, and $99 per client a month for agencies, with a minimum of 3 clients. The site says nothing can be bought yet. [1]
