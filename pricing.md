## The free month

If no fix of ours went live on your site in a calendar month, that month is credited on your next invoice. You do not need to ask. A fix counts as live only when we have fetched your public page and found its text on it. Marking a fix as done yourself does not count. A pitch to another website is never live. The first month that counts is your first full calendar month.

## Moving between plans

You can move between Holdout and Holdout Growth in Settings. Moving down deletes nothing. Questions above 24 are kept and stop being measured, a weekly limit above 5 is lowered to 5, and measuring goes back to one AI engine.

## Price changes

Anyone who signs up keeps the price and limits they signed up at. A customer who moves to another plan takes that plan's current price.
